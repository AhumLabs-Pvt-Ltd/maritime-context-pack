# Roles

Who does what around a ship's certificates. Real arrangements vary: one company can hold
several of these roles, and the flag state decides how much it delegates.

## Owner

The registered owner is the person or company named on the Certificate of Registry. The
owner is legally responsible for the ship, chooses its flag and class society, and
appoints a manager if it does not run the ship itself.

## Manager

The company that runs the ship day to day: crewing, maintenance, safety management and
often commercial operation. Under the ISM Code, the "Company" is whoever has taken on
responsibility for operating the ship, which is often the manager rather than the owner.
That company holds the Document of Compliance (DOC). The manager usually tracks
certificate expiry dates and books surveys.

## Class society

A private, technical organization that writes rules for ship structure and machinery,
approves designs, and surveys ships during construction and in service. A ship "in class"
meets those rules. Many flag states also appoint class societies as recognized
organizations (ROs) to carry out statutory surveys and issue convention certificates on
the flag's behalf. Insurers and charterers usually require a ship to be in class.

## Flag state

The country where the ship is registered. It sets national law for its ships, puts IMO
conventions into effect, and is responsible for making sure its ships comply. It issues
or authorises the statutory certificates, and it investigates casualties involving its
ships.

## Port state control (PSC)

Inspections of foreign ships by the country whose port they visit. PSC officers check
that certificates are valid and that the ship's condition, equipment and crew match them.
They can record deficiencies and, for serious problems, detain the ship until it is fixed.
Countries cooperate in regional agreements (for example the Paris MoU and Tokyo MoU) that
share inspection data and target higher-risk ships.

## How they connect

```text
Owner ──appoints──> Manager (holds DOC) ──runs──> Ship (holds SMC and other certificates)
  │                                                   ▲
  └──registers ship with──> Flag state ──authorises──> Class society / RO ──surveys──┘
                                                      Port state control ──inspects──┘
```
